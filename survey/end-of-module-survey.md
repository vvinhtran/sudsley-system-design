# End-of-module survey

The survey given after all assignments were submitted and graded. It was an online form;
completing it earned a small amount of extra credit, awarded for completion rather than
content. The paper reports its results. Items are shown with the scenario's current name,
Sudsley.

As administered, the form also collected each respondent's email address and name so the extra
credit could be awarded. If you reuse the survey for research, decide how you will separate
identity from responses before you run it.
