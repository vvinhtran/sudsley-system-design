# HW1: High-level design

**Collaborative.** Assigned after lecture 1 (`slides/1-introduction.pdf`).

## Background

Read the [Sudsley scenario and MVP brief](../../scenario/pitch-and-mvp-brief.md). The CEO has settled on the
product and the MVP requirements. Your team's job now is to propose how the system should be
built.

A high-level (or macro-level) design is the first formal step of system design. It should be
easy to follow. It identifies the necessary components (microservices, external services,
clients, and so on) without going into how each is implemented, and it gets everyone aligned
on the project's goals and who is responsible for what. It may also include UI designs, data
flow diagrams, a technology stack, or a deployment procedure.

## What to submit

1. **A system description.** Based on the product described in class and the MVP
   requirements, write a brief description of how you think the system should look and
   operate. No more than half a page.
2. **A high-level design diagram** of the Sudsley system.
   - A business or system use-case diagram is a good starting point.
   - You don't need to follow UML strictly. Make it readable and intuitive.
   - Use any tool you like, for example [draw.io](https://app.diagrams.net/),
     [Lucidchart](https://www.lucidchart.com/), or [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/).

Submit both as one PDF, one per team. Your instructor will tell you the team size and where to
submit.

Be flexible. No design is correct or incorrect.
